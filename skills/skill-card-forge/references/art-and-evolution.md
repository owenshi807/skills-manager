# Art and evolution

## Inputs and grade semantics

Accept a review handoff plus one user idea. Old art, preferred elements, creatures, and style are optional. If there is no review record, a user may request a clearly labelled grade design preview; do not imply review or award issuance.

`C`, `R`, and `SR` map to 凡、灵、天. `ssr_status: recorded` keeps 圣 / SSR while showing the current assessment separately. `supported` may produce a 圣 design preview while stating the award is not recorded. Existing legitimate 圣 achievements persist across redraws, theme changes, and added/removed inhabitants. Pure visual revision never grants, repeats, revokes, or upgrades SSR.

Use the same recognizable equipment identity for one Skill. Express grade through structure, material, space, and relationship, never recolor or brightness alone:

| Grade | Visual meaning | Effect boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡 / C | A complete, refined ordinary object with one readable function. | No foil, rare gold trim, or self-lighting. |
| 灵 / R | A legible internal mechanism or method path. | No foil, rare gold trim, or self-lighting. |
| 天 / SR | Equipment responds to environmental conditions through distinct conditional structure and space. | Equipment-only foil and thin gold trim may be used. |
| 圣 / SSR | Equipment and a personal domain share one expressive relation; the domain may be small. | Foil may cover the card except text; local motion is optional. |

Normal material reflections are allowed. Never bake rainbow foil, whole-card sweep, text, numbers, grade badges, logos, watermarks, or card frame into generated art. Card text and achievement markers belong to deterministic layout/graphic layers. Neither creatures nor spectacle change grade. A core spirit remains inside its core; a dragon, animal, or guardian has its own habitat, path, and depth.

## Static art workflow

State a direction in one sentence: **equipment + domain (if applicable) + one relation**. Then generate an original static concept with a clear equipment focus and quiet layout space. Preserve requested identity traits from an inspected reference image. An external style reference is permitted when the user explicitly gives it, but borrow visual language only: retain original equipment, no franchise characters, logos, signature weapons, or copied compositions.

The default recipe is an original adventure field-guide look. Style is replaceable, never a grade rule or closed whitelist. In particular, the same 圣 `万象重构镜` may be compared as four style recipes while retaining its original open-ring reconstruction equipment identity and domain relation:

| Recipe | Borrow only | Keep original |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pokémon TCG cel-shaded | clean cel shading, collectible-card color separation | all equipment, symbols, creatures, and setting design |
| Bruno Simon toy low-poly open world | toy-like low-poly forms, readable miniature open-world terrain | original reconstruction mirror and domain |
| HD-2D pixel volume | pixel-art surfaces, diorama depth, volumetric-light feeling | original equipment structure and scene |
| Hades hand-painted myth | bold hand-painted mythic color and graphic brushwork | original equipment, gods/characters, and iconography |

These are examples, not a whitelist. A map and card face may use different-detail representations of the same equipment identity. Do not create a new Skill per comparison; change a reusable `style_recipe` while keeping `skill_ref`, equipment identity, grade, and achievement origin intact.

Verify a generated comparison visually before calling the recipe successful. Identity means a few anchor shapes/colors, not preserving every engraved microdetail. For a toy map, explicitly specify chunky beveled geometry and matte materials in both views; simplify fine jewelry parts while preserving the same open/connected structure. Define pickup and inhabitant scale relative to a nearby explorer when map readability matters. Turn the chosen domain relation into a visible shared structure or route, rather than merely placing a creature beside the equipment. One targeted revision is better than adding more effects. Record any remaining concept-level deviations; do not call a concept sheet a production sprite, mesh, or registered layer pack.

For same-grade evolution, retain the previous art as a reference and never overwrite the only original. Say what stays and what changes in two sentences, then create one meaningful alteration in silhouette, materials, patterning, background, domain extent, inhabitants, or their relation. Simplification is valid; do not force each iteration to become brighter, bigger, or more crowded.
